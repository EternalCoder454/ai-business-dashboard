# Template: asset notice for an LGPL mod repository

Copy this into the mod repository as `ASSETS.md`, next to the LGPL `LICENSE`.

It exists because one `LICENSE` file at the root reads as covering the whole
repository. Without this, a fork reasonably assumes the art came with the code.

---

```markdown
# Assets

The code in this repository is licensed under LGPL-3.0-or-later. See LICENSE.

**The assets are not.** Textures, models, sounds, music, icons, and the mod's
logo are copyright Eterneon Studio, all rights reserved. No licence is granted
to them by the LGPL, and none is granted here.

## What that means for a fork

You may fork the code, change it, and publish your version under LGPL-3.0.

You may not ship it with the original art. Replace the assets, or ask.

The mod's name and the studio's name are trademarks and are not licensed by the
LGPL either. Name your fork something else, and do not present it as an official
Eterneon release.

## Third-party assets

Any asset not created by Eterneon Studio is listed in CREDITS.md with its own
terms, which apply above this notice for that asset.

## Asking

Permission for a specific use is often granted and costs nothing to request.
Contact details are on the official listing.

## Not an official Minecraft product

Not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or
Microsoft.
```
